# Atta + Besan Rotis — 1 person, lunch + dinner

**Type:** weekday-dish  
**Fridge life:** cooked rotis 2-3 days, but best same day · **Freezer-safe:** No, because cooked rotis are not freezer-approved in this plan  
**Active time:** 20-25 min (morning) · **Makes:** 4 rotis = 2 lunch + 2 dinner  
**Uses prepped components:** none  
**Source:** own  

*Storage rule: do **not** store dough; store cooked rotis only, as the plan notes for batch rotis.*

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           |                       Quantity |
| -------------------- | -----------------------------: |
| Atta                 |                          140 g |
| Besan                |                           40 g |
| Water                | 118-125 ml, add gradually |
| Salt                 |                 ¼ tsp optional |
| Oil in dough         |                           1 tsp |
| Dry atta for rolling |              1-2 tbsp, as needed |
| Ghee for smear       |              ½-1 tsp total optional |

## Process

1. Mix **140 g atta + 40 g besan + ¼ tsp salt**. This is roughly a **78:22 atta:besan ratio**. Keeping besan around 20-22% gives noticeable besan flavour and extra protein while retaining enough wheat gluten for the rotis to roll, hold together, and puff properly; a higher besan percentage would increase edge cracking and tearing risk.
2. Add **1 tsp oil** and rub it evenly through the flour. The small amount of oil helps keep the lower-gluten atta-besan dough pliable.
3. Add about **115 ml water gradually** while mixing. Once the dough comes together, add water **1 tsp at a time** as needed; total will usually be around **118-125 ml**. Aim for a medium-soft dough with no dry cracks, but do not make it sticky.
4. Knead for **6-7 minutes** until smooth and cohesive. It will feel slightly less stretchy than plain atta dough because besan contains no gluten; do not keep adding water or over-kneading just to make it feel identical to plain atta dough.
5. Cover and rest for **15-20 minutes**. This rest is more important than for the plain atta version because it lets both atta and besan hydrate fully and reduces cracking while rolling.
6. Knead briefly for **20-30 seconds**, then divide into **4 equal balls**.
7. Roll each ball gently with minimal dry atta. Keep the roti slightly thicker than a very thin plain atta roti and use lighter pressure near the edges. If an edge starts cracking, pinch it together before continuing rather than repeatedly dusting with flour.
8. Heat the tawa well on **medium-high heat**. Besan colours and dries faster than plain atta, so avoid leaving the roti on one side too long:

    - Side 1: **25-30 sec**, until small bubbles appear and the surface starts losing its raw look.
    - Side 2: **35-45 sec**, until scattered brown spots appear.
    - Flip again: cook **20-30 sec**, pressing the edges gently with a spatula or cloth to encourage puffing.
    - Remove as soon as cooked and puffed; overcooking makes atta-besan rotis noticeably drier and more brittle than plain atta rotis.

9. Lightly smear with optional ghee and stack the cooked rotis together so retained steam keeps them soft.
10. **Technique difference vs plain atta:** expect slightly less elasticity, greater edge-tearing risk, gentler rolling, a more important resting period, and slightly more attention on the tawa because the besan portion browns and dries faster.

## Storage for lunch + dinner

- **Lunch 2 rotis:** keep wrapped in a clean cotton cloth inside a casserole/covered box.
- **Dinner 2 rotis:** cool to warm for **30-40 minutes, never longer**, then wrap in cloth/foil, place in an airtight box, and refrigerate.
- Reheat only the **portion being eaten** on a hot tawa for about **20-30 sec per side**, until piping hot throughout.
- If the refrigerated rotis feel dry, sprinkle only a few drops of water before reheating and keep them briefly covered after heating.
- Do not store leftover atta-besan dough for later; store the cooked rotis instead.

## Nutrition note

Approximate values, including the **1 tsp oil in the dough** but excluding optional ghee:

- **Per roti:** about **165-170 kcal** and **6.5-7 g protein**.
- **All 4 rotis:** about **665-675 kcal** and **27 g protein**.
